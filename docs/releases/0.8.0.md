## Sound controls and quick access, together

- Keep everyday controls and device information in one bounded Sound workspace.
  The original device images stay at the outer edges, with horizontal volume
  controls and aligned mute, Details and program buttons between them.
- Choose Together for all sound sections, or Separate for individual controls.
  Each section can override the master; Mixed modes explains when they differ.
  Inline microphone and streaming EQ keep both ears visible, with vertical
  tone faders or eight reported bands. Fitted tinnitus controls have their own
  section. EQ edits remain drafts until Apply.
- Open the graphical menu-bar panel for batteries, runtime, volume, mute,
  SpeechBooster, program selection and connection controls. Configure its
  sections, artwork, battery label and compact or expanded layout.
- Add desktop battery and sound widgets. On macOS 26 or later, add individual
  volume, mute, SpeechBooster, app-connection and Mac-audio Control Center
  controls. The running app validates expiring requests; stale actions are
  discarded rather than replayed after reconnecting.
- Read battery runtime automatically on connection and about hourly while
  connected. Timestamped cached readings count down locally between device
  reads instead of polling continuously.
- Search native Help for features and jump directly to their page or Settings
  category. Local guidance is available in English, Brazilian Portuguese and
  Spanish.
- Fit About and Settings without inner scrolling. Settings has full-row green
  navigation, focused category tabs and optional nearby/wake/unlock connection
  automation. Automatic connection starts off; pause/resume remains explicit.

Downloads are universal for Apple Silicon and Intel and require macOS 14 or
later. The protected release workflow signs with Developer ID, notarizes and
staples the app and DMG, then verifies the downloads on a fresh Mac before
publication. Interactive widgets require the app to remain running.

The new gallery and layout checks use synthetic demo devices. Existing BLE
consumer controls retain their readback checks; physical sound changes through
every new widget and system control still need wearer confirmation. App BLE
connections and system audio are separate: Mac output selection does not force
Android/Mac audio handoff. Clinical fitting writes, new on-device programs and
firmware updates remain unimplemented. See the
[quick-access guide](https://github.com/sabino/audifern/blob/v0.8.0/docs/quick-access.md)
and [validation record](https://github.com/sabino/audifern/blob/v0.8.0/docs/quick-access-validation.md).
