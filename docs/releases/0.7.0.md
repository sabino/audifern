## A clearer listening space and a private fitting workbench

- Find everyday controls in **Sound**, programs and local presets in **Library**,
  connection details in **Devices**, and fitting tools in **Hearing profile**.
  Larger text, aligned controls and native attached sheets keep related tasks
  together, with Back and Done instead of a long collection of disclosure rows.
- Keep the original hearing-aid illustrations and Audifern mark. Appearance
  follows macOS or your preference; English, Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish
  cover the new interface, including the About and contribution screens.
- Load microphone and streaming EQ automatically when their editor opens.
  Each ear has its own progress and retry state; switching between Left and
  Both no longer cancels or duplicates the pending read. Choose tone controls
  or eight reported bands, then explicitly Apply your draft.
- Adjust volume with a native slider that submits once on release. Linked
  adjustment preserves the existing balance and checks both aids' current
  limits and readback.

- Explore recovered listening programs, adaptation, processing and gain/MPO
  parameter tables in **Hearing profile → Saved fitting**.
- Inspect fitting software version, stored rationales and tagged audiogram
  snapshots without connecting to the aids. Stored units and original exam
  dates remain unknown; local dB HL review requires external confirmation.
- Prepare a separate local plan with context, per-ear acoustics, a linked exam,
  supported rationale/adaptation preferences and program ideas. A five-step
  editor preserves private revisions and returns to your place after editing
  the audiogram.
- Preserve readable state in verified, private, immutable backups. Completed
  reads are saved incrementally; interrupted captures are labeled as partial.
- Enter 750 Hz and 1500 Hz audiogram values and use the editor in narrower
  windows. Fix the native-sheet layout that could stall when opening the
  audiogram from a plan, and keep Settings geometry stable between categories.
- Compare saved backups and export an identifier-redacted fitting summary PDF.
- Open **About Audifern** from the app menu, menu bar or Settings for the version,
  author, GPL source and optional contributions. Receiving addresses and QR
  codes are local; Mercado Pago opens only when you choose its link. No payment
  tracking, supporter lock or payment backend is included.

Downloads are universal for Apple Silicon and Intel, require macOS 14 or later,
and are Developer ID signed and Apple-notarized by the protected release
workflow. The DMG and the app inside the ZIP carry stapled Apple tickets.

Direct read-only acquisition was checked on one Intent 3 pair with release
5.0.29, HIID 4.5 and memory map 10.0.0. Each aid returned its fitting-system
record and all 25 advertised persisted end-user-setting scopes. Other profiles
are preserved without guessing their parameter layout.

These changes do not implement clinical fitting writes, audiogram-to-gain
calculation, new on-device programs, calibrated measurements, clinical restore
or firmware updates. Gain tables show raw parameter coordinates, not calibrated
acoustic responses. See the [backup guide](https://github.com/sabino/audifern/blob/v0.7.0/docs/fitting-backups.md)
and [feature map](https://github.com/sabino/audifern/blob/v0.7.0/docs/genie-feature-map.md)
for coverage and remaining work.
