# Audifern 0.9.0

- Connect and disconnect from the top of the menu-bar panel, with clear automatic-reconnect status.
- Together and Separate stay disabled until both hearing aids are connected.
- Automatic reconnection rechecks the current Mac session, recovering after a missed unlock or wake notification.
- An explicit Connect can briefly scan for your remembered aids even when automatic reconnection is off.
- Settings now includes Updates & changelog, also reachable from the Audifern menu and Help search.
- Direct-download installations support signed, verified in-app updates and a choice of when to install and reopen.
- Malt and Homebrew installations show their matching upgrade command and keep the in-app installer disabled.

Bluetooth controls and audio routing remain separate. These changes do not
install hearing-aid firmware or change clinical fittings.
